Vocus describes Horizon as a 2,000 km Perth-to-Port Hedland network with direct
connections to NEXTDC's Port Hedland and Newman edge sites. It reports 38 Tbps
of network capacity, services up to 400 Gbps and three paths out of the Pilbara:
terrestrial, subsea and via Darwin.

These are network-level and operator-reported characteristics. A candidate
service still requires contracted bandwidth, route and demarcation details,
latency measurements and failure-domain testing.
