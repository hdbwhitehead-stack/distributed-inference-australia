Leading Edge says its colocation offer includes metered PDUs, NOC monitoring,
monthly SLA reporting and 24/7 regional remote hands. It describes power,
cooling, network, security and monitoring as components of a monthly colocation
bill and advertises a 99.985% uptime SLA.

Those standard claims make a commercial enquiry possible. They do not establish
DBO1 pricing, contractual capacity, high-density integration responsibility or
the availability of a flexible-power tariff.
