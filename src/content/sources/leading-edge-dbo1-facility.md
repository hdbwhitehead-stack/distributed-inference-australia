The current facility page describes DBO1 as a Tier III-design regional data
centre in Dubbo. It lists N+1 supply redundancy, standard A/B rack feeds, UPS,
backup generation, in-row direct-expansion cooling with N+1 redundancy, dark
fibre to the Dubbo NBN point of interconnect, network services up to 100 Gbps,
and physical-security controls.

The published 750 kW and 375 kW figures are operator nameplate claims. They are
not recorded as candidate capacity observations because no source establishes
what is presently uncontracted, continuously available to this project, or
deliverable to one high-density rack at a stated electrical boundary.
