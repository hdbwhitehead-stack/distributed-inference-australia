NEXTDC describes PH1 as an operating Tier III-certified regional edge data
centre. The page lists N+1 main electrical infrastructure, N+N power-rail
redundancy, two 400 kVA backup generators, cold-aisle and in-row cooling,
diverse underground connectivity pathways, the hosted Vocus Horizon cable,
remote hands, and security and management-system certifications.

The operator's 1 MW IT-capacity and 1.5 MW facility descriptions are useful
nameplate indicators, not proof of uncontracted or continuously deliverable
power for this project. No numerical candidate capacity observation is created
from them.
