title: A depreciation deduction is not necessarily an immediate cash benefit
claim: >-
  A project company without sufficient taxable income generally carries its tax
  loss forward, so accelerated depreciation may defer tax only after the company
  becomes profitable rather than fund the initial investment.

implications:
  - The tax shield's present value depends on the timing and ownership of taxable profits.
  - A standalone pilot SPV may realise much less early cash value than a profitable tax group.
  - Debt sizing should not assume a current cash refund from depreciation.

Carry-forward losses preserve potential future deductions but delay their cash
value and introduce ownership and continuity conditions.
