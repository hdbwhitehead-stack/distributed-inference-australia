At A$11 million of eligible equipment and a 25% tax rate, four-year prime-cost
depreciation produces A$2.75 million of nominal tax deductions multiplied by the
tax rate over the asset life. At a 10% discount rate, taking the same nominal
shield over four rather than nine years adds about A$0.42 million of present
value, assuming deductions can be used immediately.
