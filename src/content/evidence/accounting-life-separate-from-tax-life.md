title: Accounting useful life is separate from tax effective life
claim: >-
  AASB 116 requires useful life to reflect the asset's expected utility to the
  entity and to be reviewed at least annually; a four-year tax life does not
  automatically justify writing the asset to zero over four years in the
  financial statements.

A fully depreciated asset can produce an apparently extreme accounting ROIC even
when its current resale value or the value of its occupied power capacity remains
material.
