DBO1 advertises dark fibre to the Dubbo NBN point of interconnect. PH1 hosts the
now-live Vocus Horizon route, for which Vocus describes three paths out of the
Pilbara. These facts justify quotes and testing; they do not establish reserved
bandwidth, route independence, measured latency or price. One fibre path may be
acceptable for a flex site; firm service needs independent capacity elsewhere.
