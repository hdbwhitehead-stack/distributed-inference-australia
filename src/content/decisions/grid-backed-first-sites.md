---
id: decision-grid-backed-first-sites
date: 2026-08-29
decision: >-
  Prioritise small, quickly energised, mostly firm or grid-backed sites with
  curtailment flexibility over renewable-only sites for initial screening.
status: active
rationale: >-
  New accelerator economics are highly sensitive to lost utilisation, and a
  flexible service may still need import capability or firm overflow.
reversible: true
revisit_when: >-
  A site demonstrates materially different hardware, energy and customer-service
  economics.
related_assumptions:
  - assumption-power-advantage-exceeds-utilisation-loss
  - assumption-local-load-captures-value
related_evidence:
  - evidence-utilisation-dominates-power-savings
---

This is the current working prioritisation stated in the cockpit handoff, not a
finding that renewable-only sites cannot work.
